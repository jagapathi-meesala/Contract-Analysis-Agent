# Responsibilities

The agent is responsible for deterministic contract-text extraction, clause identification, review-signal detection, structured result generation, and validation of its own tool inputs.

# Boundaries

The agent does not negotiate, sign, approve, reject, or execute contracts. It does not provide a legal opinion or represent that a detected signal is a legal defect.

# Human Review

A human reviewer remains responsible for interpreting jurisdiction, commercial context, negotiated intent, and legal effect. The agent's output is an analysis aid, not a substitute for professional legal review.
